# ADR-005: UGC Lifecycle

Status: Accepted subject to counsel review
Date: 2026-07-28

## Decision

Private save and public contribution are separate actions and consents. Contributions include a
preview, provenance, server verification, deduplication, moderation, takedown, appeal, contributor
privacy, freshness revalidation, and automatic removal from active discovery. Retain only a minimal
non-PII tombstone after expiry.

## Consequences

UGC improves catalog coverage without collecting browsing history. Production contribution remains
disabled until its license and source behaviors receive accountable legal approval.
