# Public Product Naming Brief

Status: Provisional development identity selected; final public approval deferred to D6
Working presentation brand: ApplyDash
Candidate domain: applydash.ai
Stable technical identifier: ai-job-apply
Accountable role: Product owner

## Provisional-use decision

Use ApplyDash and `applydash.ai` on internal presentation surfaces until a final public brand is
approved, regardless of which delivery phase contains that decision. This is not a representation
that the domain is owned, the name is legally clear, or the public-name gate is complete.

Presentation branding must come from shared configuration. Do not rename package scopes,
databases, event sources, authentication audiences, cloud resources, or repository paths when the
presentation brand changes. Do not use the candidate domain for production origins, email, OAuth,
links, or certificates until ownership and approval are recorded.

## Requirements

The public name should be pronounceable, easy to type, distinct from Jobright and other employment
products, usable internationally, and broad enough to cover discovery, tailoring, and
application execution without implying coaching or guaranteed hiring results.

Avoid names that:

- Copy competitor naming, branding, or visual identity
- Promise jobs, interviews, offers, or guaranteed outcomes
- Imply employer affiliation or official ATS status
- Depend on a difficult spelling or unavailable primary domain

## Screening gate

For each finalist, record:

- Exact and confusingly similar product/company search
- USPTO and relevant state trademark search prepared for counsel
- Primary and defensible alternate domain availability
- Apple App Store, Google Play, and Chrome Web Store conflicts
- Social/account-handle conflicts
- Linguistic and accessibility review
- Counsel disposition and product-owner approval

D0 may use ApplyDash through the swappable presentation-brand contract without treating it as a
legally cleared or owned public identity. Final public naming is tracked by D6-012 and must complete
before production origins, OAuth consent branding, email domains, browser/mobile stores, paid
release, or public marketing use the name.
