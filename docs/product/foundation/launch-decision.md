# Launch Decision Record

Status: Partially approved
Decision date: 2026-07-28
Accountable role: Product owner

## Approved launch focus

- Geography: nationwide United States; counsel must enumerate state, DC, territorial, and other
  jurisdictional obligations before launch
- Language: English
- Minimum user age: 18
- Initial audience: US-based early- and mid-career software, data, and product professionals
- Core job types: full-time, part-time, contract, and internship knowledge-work roles
- Product objective: reduce active user time per relevant, truthful, tailored, confirmed application
- Application history boundary: technical state through confirmed submission only
- Monetization: Free plan and one paid Pro plan; no additional paid tiers

## Explicit exclusions

- Career, interview, or other coaching
- Mock interviews, interview preparation, or interview-question generation
- Inbox or calendar reading
- Recruiter-response, interview, offer, rejection, or other hiring-outcome tracking
- Employer visibility into candidate profiles without separate explicit consent

is complete for launch focus and internal positioning; D6-012 owns final public-name/domain
screening and approval before public release.
