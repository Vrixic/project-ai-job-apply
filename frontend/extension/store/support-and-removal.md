# Support, withdrawal, and removal disclosure — draft

The web source now includes a static `/extension-support` route with accurate local delivery-state,
withdrawal, appeal, candidate-rights, and privacy guidance. Local desktop and mobile checks pass,
but the route is not yet accepted as a durable hosted store URL and no monitored public support,
rights-request, or takedown channel is approved for this candidate. No placeholder address or
personal contact should be placed in a store listing.

Before distribution, the release owner must provide and test:

- a deployment and availability check for the `/extension-support` route plus a monitored support
  channel;
- authenticated removal of local blocked/terminal queue records;
- authenticated contribution status and withdrawal without exposing contributor identity;
- employer/provider and urgent safety takedown intake with requester verification;
- owner-scoped export/deletion coverage and documented restoration handling;
- appeal availability under the exact approved policy version; and
- acknowledgement/escalation routing, abuse protection, and audit evidence.

Uninstalling the extension is not a substitute for withdrawing a previously acknowledged server
contribution. The current extension does not expose a private-save action. Store copy must explain
the contribution boundary using the final implemented behavior and approved policy.

Until the hosted route, remaining controls, and a real monitored channel exist, the only truthful
operational statement is that the extension is not available for public use or store distribution.
