# Trust Service

Owns UGC moderation, source and contributor reputation, stale/fake job signals, malicious content handling, abuse limits, takedowns, and appeals.

## Planned subfolders

- `moderation/` — Automated and human contribution review.
- `reputation/` — Source/contributor evidence and confidence.
- `job-safety/` — Scam, redirect, payment-request, and phishing signals.
- `abuse/` — Spam, automation, and account-behavior controls.
- `takedowns/` — Employer/copyright/privacy removal workflows.
- `appeals/` — Reviewable decisions and supporting evidence.
- `tests/` — False-positive, evasion, moderation, and audit tests.

Trust decisions are explainable and appealable and must not become a hidden candidate-screening system.
