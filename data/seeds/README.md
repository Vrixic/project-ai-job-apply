# Development Seeds

Contains deterministic synthetic data for local development, demos, previews, and integration tests.

## Planned subfolders

- `users/` — Synthetic candidate profiles and preferences.
- `companies/` — Fictional or safe public company references.
- `jobs/` — Synthetic canonical jobs and sources.
- `applications/` — Synthetic preparation, review, submission, failure, and confirmation states.
- `billing/` — Free/Pro and subscription lifecycle scenarios.
- `trust/` — Moderation and stale/fake job scenarios.

Seeds must be repeatable, clearly non-production, and safe to reset.
