# Taxonomies

Contains versioned controlled vocabularies and mappings used for job normalization, profile interpretation, search, and matching.

## Planned subfolders

- `skills/` — Skill aliases, relationships, and categories.
- `titles/` — Job-title aliases, families, and adjacent roles.
- `seniority/` — Level mappings and ambiguity rules.
- `industries/` — Industry hierarchy and aliases.
- `locations/` — Geographic aliases and work-model vocabulary.
- `employment/` — Employment-type and schedule mappings.
- `tests/` — Integrity, cycle, duplicate, and migration checks.

Taxonomy changes are versioned and evaluated because they can materially change recommendations.
